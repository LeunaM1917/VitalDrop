Why timing matters

In the wake of natural disasters like typhoons, the gap between a need and its fulfillment can be life or death. Traditional communication loops are often fragmented, leading to critical delays in blood delivery.

VitalDrop closes these gaps by unifying inventory data across regions, ensuring that even when local supplies are compromised, the nearest verified alternative is identified and mobilized instantly.

How VitalDrop works

Seamless logistics from the first request to the final audit.

Request & triage

Hospitals enter needs via an AI-assisted portal that prioritizes based on urgency and blood type rarity.

Inventory visibility

Real-time tracking of blood banks ensures we know exactly where every unit is located across the network.

Donor & network activation

Smart notifications are sent to the most eligible donors in the vicinity, bypassing noise to find heroes.

Fulfillment & audit trail

Every drop is logged. A complete blockchain-backed audit trail ensures safety and accountability at every step.
